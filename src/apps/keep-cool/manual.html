Keep It Cool

Field manual for the fictional two-player drill. Ask the operator to read each panel aloud. Use this sheet to tell them which controls to operate and in what order. An interlock resets the current panel and costs 15 seconds.

01 / Emergency power

Ask for the feed colour and load. Find the reserve in the table. For Blue, press the reserve, then the isolator. For Amber or White, press the isolator, then the reserve.

03 / Heat removal

Ask the operator to read probes A and B. Start pump K if the signals match (both steady or both pulsing). Start pump M if they differ.

Once flow returns, wait five seconds for temperature confirmation. The countdown must still be running at confirmation.
